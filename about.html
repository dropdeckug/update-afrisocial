ABOUT US

Afrisocial is a social platform founded by Adinkwu Ugochukwu  on November 2025 and launched publicly on February 2nd, 2026. It was built by Africans, for Africans — designed to connect people, amplify voices, and showcase the richness of African culture to the world.

The Problem

For years, Africans have used global platforms that don’t fully represent their stories, culture, or opportunities. Local creators often struggle with visibility, recognition, and fair rewards

The Solution

Afrisocial was created to change that, giving Africans a platform where their voices matter, their creativity is valued, and their stories are seen and celebrated.

What Afrisocial Offers

Share moments through posts and stories
Discover engaging videos on Vybze
Explore African culture and conversations on AfriExplore
Connect with people across the continent
Grow and earn as a creator

Who It’s For

Afrisocial is for creators, entrepreneurs, storytellers, and everyday people who want to connect, express themselves, and be part of a growing African digital community.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build Africa’s leading social platform, one that creates opportunities, empowers creators, and drives economic growth across the continent.

Our Mission

Beyond connection, Afrisocial aims to create jobs, support talent, and contribute to Africa’s digital future.
