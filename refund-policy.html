Afrisocial Refund Policy

📅 Effective Date: 24 May 2026

1. Digital Purchases Are Generally Non-Refundable

All purchases of:

  • Stars
  • Digital gifts
  • Creator support items
  • Promotional boosts
  • Premium digital features

are considered final and non-refundable once successfully processed and delivered to your account.

Because digital products are instantly accessible and usable, refunds are normally not issued after completion.

2. Cases Where Refunds May Be Considered

Afrisocial may review refund requests in situations such as:

  • Duplicate transactions
  • Unauthorized transactions
  • Failed purchases where Stars were not delivered
  • Technical errors caused by Afrisocial systems
  • Incorrect billing caused by system malfunction

Refund approvals are handled case-by-case.

3. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will NOT be issued for:

  • Gifts already sent to another user
  • Used Stars
  • Buyer remorse
  • Account bans caused by policy violations
  • Accidental purchases caused by user negligence
  • Referral reward reversals
  • Currency fluctuations or payment provider charges

4. Chargeback Abuse

Users must not abuse payment disputes or chargebacks.

Afrisocial may:

  • Suspend wallet access
  • Freeze accounts
  • Reverse balances
  • Permanently ban users involved in fraudulent disputes

Fraudulent chargebacks may also be reported to payment providers or authorities.

5. Processing Time

Approved refunds may take:

  • 3–15 business days depending on payment provider
  • Additional processing time from banks or card issuers

Afrisocial does not control external banking delays.

6. Wallet Balance Corrections

Afrisocial reserves the right to:

  • Correct wallet errors
  • Reverse fraudulent transactions
  • Remove improperly obtained Stars
  • Freeze suspicious balances during investigations

7. How to Request a Refund

Users may contact Afrisocial support with:

  • Account username
  • Transaction ID
  • Payment receipt
  • Description of the issue
Submitting a request does not guarantee approval.

Afrisocial may update this Refund Policy at any time to improve platform protection, financial security, and compliance.

Continued use of Afrisocial means you accept the latest version of this policy.
