Afrisocial Wallet & Stars Policy

📅 Effective Date: 24 May 2026

1. What Are Stars?

Stars are Afrisocial’s virtual digital currency used for:

  • Sending gifts
  • Supporting creators
  • Participating in platform features
  • Unlocking future premium experiences
Stars have no real-world cash value outside Afrisocial.

2. Wallet Usage Rules

Users may:

  • Purchase Stars legally
  • Send gifts to creators and users
  • Earn rewards through approved systems
  • Use Stars within Afrisocial features

Users may NOT:

  • Sell Stars outside Afrisocial
  • Transfer Stars illegally
  • Exchange Stars for unauthorized services
  • Use stolen payment methods
  • Exploit bugs or system loopholes

3. Referral Program Rules

Users may earn referral rewards through legitimate invitations.

Prohibited behavior includes:

  • Fake accounts
  • Self-referrals
  • Bot referrals
  • Fraudulent signups
  • Manipulated activity

Afrisocial may reverse rewards gained through abuse.

4. Gifts & Creator Support

When users send gifts:

  • Stars are deducted from the sender’s wallet
  • Gifts may be displayed publicly
  • Transactions are considered final

Afrisocial may limit or remove gifting privileges for abuse or suspicious activity.

5. Wallet Security

Users are responsible for:

  • Securing their account
  • Protecting passwords and login credentials
  • Monitoring wallet activity

Afrisocial is not responsible for losses caused by:

  • Shared accounts
  • Weak passwords
  • User negligence
  • Unauthorized access outside platform control

6. Fraud Prevention

Afrisocial may monitor wallet activity to detect:

  • Payment fraud
  • Chargeback abuse
  • Fake transactions
  • Suspicious gifting patterns
  • Referral manipulation

Accounts under investigation may experience:

  • Temporary wallet freezes
  • Withdrawal restrictions
  • Feature suspension

7. Payment Providers

Wallet purchases may be processed through third-party payment providers.

Afrisocial is not responsible for:

  • Banking delays
  • Provider outages
  • External transaction fees
  • Currency conversion charges

8. Account Suspension & Wallet Access

If an account violates Afrisocial policies, Afrisocial may:

  • Restrict wallet features
  • Freeze Stars balances
  • Reverse fraudulent rewards
  • Suspend or permanently ban accounts

9. No Cash Value

Stars:

  • Are not legal tender
  • Cannot normally be converted into cash
  • Cannot be transferred outside approved Afrisocial systems
  • Do not represent ownership or investment assets
Stars have no monetary value outside the Afrisocial platform.

Afrisocial may modify wallet features, Stars systems, gifting mechanics, or referral structures at any time.

Continued use of Afrisocial Wallet services means you agree to the latest version of this policy.
